# Shipping Policy

Standard shipping typically takes 3-5 business days after your order ships. Most orders leave our warehouse within 1-2 business days of being placed.

Once your order ships, you will receive a confirmation email with a tracking number so you can follow your package while it is in transit.

If your order does not arrive within the expected delivery window, please contact support so we can investigate and, if needed, escalate the issue to our logistics team.
