# Damaged or Lost Items

If your package arrives damaged, please contact support within 7 days of delivery with photos of the damaged item and packaging. We will arrange a free replacement or a full refund, whichever you prefer.

If a package is marked as delivered but you cannot find it, wait 24 hours since packages are occasionally scanned early, and check with neighbors or your building's front desk. If it still cannot be located, contact support and we will file a claim with the carrier and send a replacement or refund.
